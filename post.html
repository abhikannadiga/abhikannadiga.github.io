How to Choose the Best Engineering Project

Good projects help you score high and improve your practical knowledge. Here are some tips to choose the right one.

1. Choose Based on Your Interest

Pick a topic that matches your skills — coding, electronics, IoT, AI, embedded systems, etc.

2. Check Availability of Components

Make sure sensors, modules, or software required for your project are easily available.

3. Pick a Unique & Practical Topic

Teachers prefer innovative projects that solve real problems.

4. Make Sure You Can Explain It

Select a project you can confidently explain in viva and presentation.
